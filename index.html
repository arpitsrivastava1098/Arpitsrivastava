Social Development & Child Protection

Building Knowledge. Strengthening Systems. Protecting Children.

Dedicated to driving meaningful change, this initiative centers on building robust child protection systems, safeguarding rights, and creating environments where every young life can thrive. From advocating against child labor and early marriage to navigating complex legal frameworks like the POCSO Act, the objective is to translate field insights into actionable, real-world solutions. By equipping communities, educators, and organizations with practical tools and knowledge, we can proactively shield children from abuse, neglect, and emerging digital threats while prioritizing their mental well-being.


This platform also provides access to professional resources, IEC materials, training modules, programme knowledge and direct support from experts working across different development sectors.
