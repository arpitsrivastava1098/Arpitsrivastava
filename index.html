About Me

Arpit Srivastava

Social Development Professional • Child Protection • Government Systems Strengthening • Training • Data & Evidence

Professional Profile

I am a social development professional with a strong focus on child protection, child rights, programme management, government systems strengthening, capacity building, data management, monitoring and evidence-based programme planning.

My work is centred on converting field-level learning and data into practical systems, tools, training modules and programme decisions that can be implemented through government and community structures.

Child Protection & Rights

I have deep working knowledge of child protection systems, prevention-based approaches, case management, risk identification, referral, community monitoring and convergence with government schemes and services.

I also develop practical IEC materials, training modules and SOP-oriented resources for children, frontline workers, community structures and government stakeholders.

Government Systems & Convergence

I work on strengthening coordination between village, block and district-level systems, with emphasis on CWPC/BCWPC/DCPC structures, reporting, review mechanisms, Panchayat-level data and departmental convergence.

My approach focuses on making systems functional, measurable and sustainable rather than creating parallel structures.

Training & Capacity Building

I am a Master Trainer with experience in designing and facilitating capacity-building processes for government functionaries, frontline workers, community representatives and other stakeholders.

My strengths include module development, training planning, facilitation, government coordination and translating complex programme requirements into simple field-level learning.

Data, MIS & Evidence

I work with programme data, MIS, monitoring frameworks, reporting systems and evidence-based analysis to support planning and decision-making.

I am particularly interested in improving data flow, data quality, dashboards, review formats and the use of evidence for corrective action.

My Approach

My professional approach combines field understanding, government coordination, structured planning, training and data. I focus on solutions that are practical for frontline implementation and can be integrated into existing institutional systems.

My core interest is building stronger child protection systems that improve prevention, early identification, response, referral and long-term institutional ownership.
